## Geometric Progression

- A **geometric progression** is a [[Sequence Definition#Sequence|sequence]] of the form $a, ar, ar^2, \ldots, ar^n, \ldots$ where the initial term $a$ and the common ratio $r$ are real numbers.
- It is a discrete analogue of the exponential function $f(x)  ar^x$.

> [!Example]
> Find the formula of the following geometric progression if we start at $n = 0$:
> 
> $1, -1, 1, -1, 1, \ldots$
> 
> $b_n = (1)^n$

## Arithmetic Progression

- An **arithmetic progression** is a [[Sequence Definition#Sequence|sequence]] of the form $a, a + d, a + 2d, a + nd, \ldots$ where the initial term $a$ and the common difference $d$ are real numbers.
- It is a discrete analogue of the linear function $f(x) = dx +a$.

> [!Example]
> Find the formula of the following arithmetic progression if we start at $n = 0$:
> 
> $-1, 3, 7, 11, \ldots$
> 
> $s_n = 4n - 1$
