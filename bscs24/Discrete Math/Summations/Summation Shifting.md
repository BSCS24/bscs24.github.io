## Shifting the Index

- This is often done when two sums need to be added but their [[Summation Definition#^index|indices]] of summation do not match.
- When **shifting an index** of summation, make the appropriate changes in the corresponding [[Summation Definition#^summand|summand]].

> [!Example]
> Suppose the [[Summation Definition#Summation|summation]] is $\sum_{j=1}^{5}j^2$ but we want the [[Summation Definition#^index|index]] to be between 0 to 4.
> 
> Let $k = j - 1$, and the term $j^2$ becomes $(k + 1)^2$.
> 
> $$\sum_{j=1}^{5}j^2 = \sum_{k=0}^{4}(k + 1)^2$$
