## Summation

We use the notations

$\sum_{j=m}^{n}{\large a}_{j} \quad \text{or} \quad \sum\nolimits_{j=m}^{n}{\large a}_{j} \quad \text{or} \quad \sum\nolimits_{1\leq j\leq n}^{}{\large a}_{j}$

to represent ${\large a}_{m} + {\large a}_{m+1} + \ldots + {\large a}_{n}$ where:

- $j$ = index ^index
- $m$ = lower limit
- $n$ = upper limit
- ${\large a}_{j}$ = summand ^summand

> [!Example]
> Express the **sum** of the first 100 [[Set Definitions#Element|elements]] of the [[Sequence Definition#Sequence|sequence]] $\{ {\large a}_{n} \}$ where ${\large a}_{n} = 1$ for $n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$
> 
> lower limit = $1$, upper limit = $100$, $\implies \sum_{j=1}^{100} \frac{1}{j}$

> [!Question]
> What is the value of $\sum_{j=1}^{5}j^2$?
> > [!Todo]- Answer
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> >  & \sum_{j=1}^{5}j^2 = 1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + 4^2 + 5^2 \\
> >  & \phantom{\sum_{j=1}^{5}j^2} = 1 + 4 + 9 + 16 + 25 \\
> >  & \phantom{\sum_{j=1}^{5}j^2} = 55
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$

> [!Question]
> What is the value of $\sum_{k=4}^{8}(-1)^k$?
> > [!Todo]- Answer
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> >  & \sum_{k=4}^{8}(-1)^k = (-1)^4 + (-1)^5 + (-1)^6 + (-1)^7 + (-1)^8 \\
> >  & \phantom{\sum_{k=4}^{8}(-1)^k} = 1 + (-1) + 1 + (-1) + 1 \\
> >  & \phantom{\sum_{k=4}^{8}(-1)^k} = 1
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
